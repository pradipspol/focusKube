# FocusKube Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** **25-Aug-2026**

FocusKube is a free, open-source desktop application for exploring and operating Kubernetes clusters. FocusKube is not provided as a hosted web service, and the FocusKube maintainers do not collect personal information, telemetry, analytics, or advertising data through the application.

## Information we collect

We do not collect, receive, sell, or share personal information through FocusKube. In particular, FocusKube does not collect:

- Email addresses or phone numbers for registration or marketing
- Names, contact details, or account profiles
- Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry
- Advertising identifiers or tracking technologies
- Payment or billing information

FocusKube is free to use and does not display advertisements or require a subscription or payment.

## Information stored on your device

FocusKube may store application settings, temporary session information, and Kubernetes-related configuration or credentials locally on your device so that the desktop application can operate. Depending on how you use the application, this may include kubeconfig data, cloud-provider configuration, locally entered desktop identity information, selected contexts, and locally recorded observability data.

This information remains on your device and is not sent to FocusKube maintainers. You control it through your operating system, Kubernetes configuration, and the files created by the application. Uninstalling the application may not remove every user-data file; check your platform's application-data directory if you want to remove locally stored data completely.

## Connections made by the application

FocusKube connects directly from your computer to services you choose to use, such as:

- Kubernetes clusters and their APIs
- Azure or AWS services when you use the corresponding integration
- Local command-line tools such as `kubectl`, `helm`, Azure CLI, AWS CLI, or Azure `kubelogin`

Those services may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. FocusKube does not control how those services process data. FocusKube does not send data to a FocusKube-operated web server or analytics provider.

## Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or share information collected through FocusKube because we do not collect information through the application. Information that you choose to send to a Kubernetes cluster or third-party cloud provider is governed by your configuration and that provider's policies.

## Children's privacy

FocusKube does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Security

You are responsible for protecting your device, kubeconfig files, cloud credentials, cluster access, and any other locally stored data used with FocusKube. Do not grant FocusKube more Kubernetes or cloud permissions than are necessary for your intended use.

## Changes to this policy

If the privacy practices of FocusKube change, this policy will be updated before or when those changes are released. The effective date at the top of this document indicates the current version.

## Contact

For privacy questions, please open an issue in the FocusKube repository:

<https://github.com/pradipspol/focusKube/issues>

This policy describes the FocusKube desktop application distributed by the FocusKube maintainers. It does not describe the privacy practices of Kubernetes, Azure, AWS, GitHub, or any other third-party service.
